# Arc Nigeria — Ecosystem Growth Map (DRAFT v3, pending approval)

Scope: everything needed to grow **Arc** (Circle's USDC-gas Layer 1) in Nigeria. That covers builders, education, projects and funding, onchain adoption, users and liquidity, BD, content, brand, community, events, jobs, policy and trust, operations and data. **Arc Studio** is the builder on-ramp inside Pillar 1.

All numeric targets are **placeholders to agree on**, not benchmarks. Partner and competitor names are **target/watch lists**, not existing relationships.

---

## 1. Strategy layer

### North Star
**Monthly Active Nigerian Ecosystem Participants (MAEP)**: unique people or orgs with ≥1 qualifying action in the month: shipped or updated an app, transacted on a Nigerian Arc app, completed a course, attended or hosted an event, published content, got hired or paid for a bounty, received a grant, or signed or advanced a partnership.

### Outcome metrics (MAEP must turn into these)
1. **Nigerian USDC volume on Arc** (monthly)
2. **Live Nigerian apps on Arc mainnet** with weekly activity
3. **Nigerian funded projects** (grants + external capital raised)

### Guardrails (growth that doesn't count)
- Sybil/fake accounts, airdrop farming, bot engagement
- Event attendance with zero follow-through
- Volume from wash trading or a single wallet cluster
- Negative sentiment spikes, scams using the Arc name

### Composite scores
- **Ecosystem Health Index**: a weighted score across pillars (weights to agree).
- **City Health Index**: the same score per city/campus, to compare Lagos vs Abuja vs Enugu and so on.

### OKRs & targets
Quarterly objectives → key results per pillar → owner → weekly progress → status (on track / at risk / off track).

## 2. Pillars

Each pillar has a funnel, KPIs, levers, an owner, and a target per phase.

### P1. Builders, Arc Studio & DevRel
- **Arc Studio lifecycle:** Aware → Signed up → First prompt → First app generated → Testnet → Exported → Mainnet live → Transacting → Retained/Advocate.
- **Developer funnel (beyond Studio):** Docs visit → SDK/App Kit install → First contract/testnet deploy → Mainnet → Repeat deployer.
- **KPIs:** NG sign-ups, stage conversion %, time-to-first-app, testnet→mainnet rate, monthly active developers, 30/90-day retention, Circle product usage (USDC, CCTP, Gateway, Wallets, App Kits, Arc Portal).
- **DevRel ops:** office hours, support tickets (volume, first-response time, resolution time), docs gaps, Nigeria prompt template packs.

### P2. Education & Talent Pipeline
- **Funnel:** Enrolled → Started → Completed → Certified → Built something → Placed/funded.
- **Programs:** university curricula, bootcamps, online courses, fellowships, train-the-trainer, non-dev tracks (product, design, marketing, BD for Web3).
- **KPIs:** enrolments, completion rate, certification, % going on to build, campus partners, instructors/mentors, women/underrepresented participation.

### P3. Projects, Startups & Funding
- **Project lifecycle:** Idea → Prototype → Testnet → Mainnet → Users → Revenue → Funded → Scaling.
- **Grants funnel:** Applied → Reviewed → Approved → Milestone 1…n paid → Completed / churned.
- **KPIs:** projects by stage and category (payments, remittance, savings/ajo, DeFi, creator, gaming, B2B, RWA), hackathon project survival at 30/90/180 days, grants disbursed and milestones hit, external capital raised, VC/accelerator intros and conversions.

### P4. Onchain Usage & Product Adoption
- **KPIs:** Nigerian apps live, weekly active apps, unique wallets, transactions, USDC volume (total and per app), contract deployments, CCTP/bridge flows into Arc, Arc Portal traffic from NG, top apps leaderboard, app retention.
- **Quality filters:** sybil checks, wallet-cluster concentration.

### P5. Users, Liquidity & Real-World Access
How everyday Nigerians get in and out.
- **On/off-ramps:** naira↔USDC-on-Arc availability, partner coverage, spreads/fees, time-to-settle, KYC pass rate, ramp volume.
- **Wallets & exchanges:** which wallets and Nigerian exchanges support USDC on the Arc network (deposit/withdraw); status tracker per venue.
- **Merchants/SMEs:** onboarded → first payment → monthly active merchants → merchant volume.
- **Remittance corridors:** inbound diaspora flows (UK, US, Canada, UAE, others), volume per corridor, cost vs traditional rails.
- **End-user funnel:** Aware → Wallet created → First USDC → First Arc transaction → Repeat user.

### P6. Business Development & Partnerships
- **Pipeline:** Identified → Contacted → Meeting → Proposal → Negotiation → Signed → Integrating → Live → Expanding (or Lost, with reason).
- **Partner types:** fintech/PSPs, exchanges and ramps, wallets, remittance, banks/MFBs, merchants/marketplaces, telcos, hubs/accelerators, universities, media, VCs, government/agency, infra (RPC, oracles, indexers, auditors).
- **KPIs:** pipeline value and count by stage/type, win rate, time-to-close, live integrations, volume and users per partner, partner health (active/at-risk/dormant), co-marketing commitments delivered.

### P7. Content & Social
- **Formats:** X threads, YouTube tutorials, short video (TikTok/Reels/Shorts), Spaces/podcasts, newsletter, blog/case studies, docs translations, Pidgin/Yoruba/Igbo/Hausa explainers, memes.
- **Funnel:** Planned → Produced → Published → Reach → Engagement → Click → Sign-up / RSVP.
- **KPIs:** output vs calendar, impressions, engagement rate, CTR, attributed sign-ups, follower growth per platform, best-performing formats and topics, creator/KOL program (creators, posts, cost, results).

### P8. Brand, PR & Market Intelligence
- **PR:** press mentions, tier-1 Nigerian and pan-African coverage, interviews, speaking slots.
- **Sentiment & social listening:** mention volume, sentiment, top narratives, emerging issues.
- **Share of voice / competitors:** Arc vs other chains and stablecoin rails active in Nigeria on developers, events, mentions, partnerships and grants (watch list to agree).
- **Brand assets:** approved logos, messaging, local brand guidelines.

### P9. Community & Ambassadors
- **Community funnel:** Joined → Active → Contributor → Leader.
- **Ambassador lifecycle:** Applied → Accepted → Onboarded → Active → Top tier / Offboarded; tasks completed, points, rewards paid, builders and participants referred.
- **Surfaces:** Discord/Telegram/WhatsApp, city chapters, campus clubs, women-in-Web3 groups.
- **KPIs:** members, weekly active, contributors, chapters, ambassador output, NPS/sentiment, toxicity/moderation incidents.

### P10. Events
- **Types:** build nights, hackathons, meetups, campus tours, demo days, workshops, partner/BD dinners, conferences (own booth, talks, side events), online events.
- **Funnel:** Planned → RSVP → Attended → Signed up / joined → Built / partnered / hired.
- **KPIs:** events by city/type, RSVP→attendance rate, cost per attendee, cost per activated participant, post-event conversion at 7/30 days, speaker/partner involvement, satisfaction score.

### P11. Jobs, Bounties & Opportunities
- **Job board funnel:** Role posted → Applicants → Shortlisted → Interviewed → Hired.
- **Bounties funnel:** Posted → Claimed → Submitted → Accepted → Paid (USDC).
- **KPIs:** open roles by function and seniority, hiring companies, applicants per role, time-to-hire, placements, bounty completion rate, USDC paid out, salary/rate benchmarks, talent directory size.

### P12. Policy, Regulation, Trust & Safety
- **Regulatory tracker:** SEC Nigeria, CBN, NITDA, FIRS, state governments; rule changes, consultations, licensing status of partners, engagement log.
- **Compliance checklist** per campaign/partner (sign-off before launch).
- **Trust & safety:** scam and impersonation reports, fake tokens/sites using the Arc name, takedowns, user-education content, incident response time.
- **Risk register:** risk, likelihood, impact, owner, mitigation, status.

### P13. Product Feedback Loop (to Circle/Arc)
- Feature requests, bugs, docs gaps, localization needs, ramp/partner blockers from Nigeria.
- Votes/frequency, status (logged → acknowledged → shipped), and what shipped because of Nigeria feedback.

## 5. Flywheel

Content, PR and events → awareness → community and education build skills → builders ship (Studio/DevRel) → projects get funded → BD brings ramps, merchants and partners → users transact onchain → jobs and bounties keep talent earning → successes become content, ambassadors and mentors → more awareness. Policy/trust and the feedback loop keep the flywheel safe and the product improving.

## 6. Geography & segments

- **Cities:** Lagos (anchor), Abuja, Port Harcourt, Enugu, Ibadan; wave 2 Kano, Benin, Kaduna, Uyo, Ilorin, Jos.
- **Campuses:** UNILAG, OAU, UI, UNN, Covenant, FUTA, ABU, UNIBEN, LASU and more.
- **Diaspora:** UK, US, Canada and UAE for remittance and talent.
- **Segments:** student devs, pro Web3 devs, Web2 devs, non-technical founders, creators, fintechs/businesses, merchants/SMEs, job seekers, end users, diaspora senders.
