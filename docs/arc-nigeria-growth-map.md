# Arc Nigeria — Ecosystem Growth Map (DRAFT v2, pending approval)

Scope: all growth, marketing and distribution for **Arc** (Circle's USDC-gas Layer 1) in Nigeria: builders, content, community, business development, jobs and talent, onchain usage, and events. **Arc Studio** is one pillar inside this.

All numeric targets are **placeholders to agree on**, not benchmarks.

---

## 1. What "growth" means

**North Star (proposed):** *Monthly Active Nigerian Ecosystem Participants* — unique people or orgs who did at least one meaningful thing in the month: shipped or updated an app, transacted on a Nigerian Arc app, attended or hosted an event, published content, got hired or completed a bounty, or signed or extended a partnership.

**Outcome check:** *Nigerian USDC volume on Arc* and *live Nigerian apps on mainnet*. Activity has to turn into real usage.

Every pillar below feeds the North Star and has its own funnel, KPIs and owner.

---

## 2. The eight pillars

### P1. Builders & Arc Studio
Developer supply and the product on-ramp.

**Arc Studio lifecycle:** Aware → Signed up → First prompt → First app generated → Testnet → Exported → Mainnet live → Transacting → Retained/Advocate.

- **KPIs:** NG sign-ups, stage conversion %, time-to-first-app, mainnet deploys, 30/90-day builder retention.
- **Levers:** Nigeria prompt template packs, build nights, office hours, testnet-to-mainnet challenges.

### P2. Content & Media
Awareness, education, narrative.

- **Formats:** X threads, YouTube tutorials, short-form video, Twitter Spaces, newsletter, blog/case studies, Pidgin and Yoruba/Igbo/Hausa explainers.
- **Funnel:** Published → Reach → Engagement → Click-through → Sign-up / event RSVP.
- **KPIs:** pieces/week, impressions, engagement rate, CTR, content-attributed sign-ups, follower growth per platform, creator program output.
- **Levers:** creator/KOL program, builder spotlights, "Built on Arc in Naija" series, content calendar.

### P3. Community
Retention and word of mouth.

- **Surfaces:** Discord/Telegram/WhatsApp, city chapters, campus clubs.
- **Funnel:** Joined → Active (posts/attends) → Contributor → Leader/Ambassador.
- **KPIs:** members, weekly active members, contributors, chapter count, NPS/sentiment.
- **Levers:** ambassador program, city leads, campus chapters, recognition and leaderboards.

### P4. Business Development & Partnerships
Distribution and real USDC flows.

- **Partner types:** fintechs and payment companies, exchanges and on/off-ramps, remittance players, merchants/SMEs, banks, hubs and accelerators, universities, media, VCs.
- **Pipeline:** Identified → Contacted → Meeting → Proposal → Signed → Integrated → Live/transacting.
- **KPIs:** pipeline by stage and type, win rate, time-to-close, live integrations (App Kits: payments, onramp, bridging, swaps), USDC volume per partner.

### P5. Jobs & Talent
Keep builders earning in the ecosystem.

- **Surfaces:** Arc Nigeria job board, bounties, grants, fellowships, talent directory.
- **Funnel:** Role/bounty posted → Applicants → Interviews/submissions → Hired/paid.
- **KPIs:** open roles, applicants per role, placements, bounty completion rate, USDC paid out, hiring partners.

### P6. Onchain Usage & Ecosystem Apps
The proof that growth is real.

- **KPIs:** Nigerian apps live (directory), weekly active apps, unique wallets, transactions, USDC volume, Arc Portal traffic from NG, top apps leaderboard.
- **Levers:** co-marketing for top apps, user campaigns via partners, ecosystem directory.

### P7. Events
Offline conversion engine.

- **Types:** build nights, hackathons, meetups, campus tours, demo days, partner/BD dinners, conference presence.
- **Funnel:** RSVP → Attended → Signed up / joined community → Shipped / partnered.
- **KPIs:** events by city and type, attendance rate, cost per attendee, post-event conversion.

### P8. Growth Ops & Experiments
How it all gets measured and improved.

- Campaigns and UTM attribution, channel mix and spend, cost per activated participant, experiment log (hypothesis → result), weekly growth review.

---

## 3. How the pillars connect (flywheel)

Content and events create awareness → community holds attention → builders ship (Studio) → BD brings partners and users → onchain usage grows → jobs and bounties keep talent paid → successful builders become content, ambassadors and mentors → more awareness.

---

## 4. Geography & segments

- **Cities:** Lagos (anchor), Abuja, Port Harcourt, Enugu, Ibadan; second wave Kano, Benin, Kaduna, Uyo.
- **Campuses:** UNILAG, OAU, UI, UNN, Covenant, FUTA, ABU and others.
- **Segments:** student devs, pro Web3 devs, non-technical founders, creators, fintechs/businesses, job seekers, end users.

Compliance: partner and consumer campaigns get checked against current SEC Nigeria and CBN rules before launch.

---

## 5. Phasing (proposed)

| Phase | Window | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| **Seed** | Days 0–30 | Tracking and dashboard live; content calendar; Lagos community + first build nights; first 20 BD targets; job board launch |
| **Activate** | Days 31–90 | 5 city chapters; hackathon; creator program; 3 partner pilots; first bounty round |
| **Scale** | Days 91–180 | Campus chapters; grants/fellowship; 10+ live integrations; ambassador program at scale |
