# Hashliquid growth map (Africa)

Independent growth, marketing and distribution work for hashliquid.com, done before pitching for a role.
Live dashboard: https://claude.ai/artifact/7y6nnZSwFRX8KPriUfpVAa (source in `dashboard/`).

## North stars (approved)
1. **Weekly Funded Active Users (WFAU):** users I brought in who have deposited and made at least one transaction that week.
2. **Weekly volume (USD):** what those users moved that week.

Only users I can trace to my links, referral codes or community groups count. I have no access to Hashliquid's internal data.

Supporting KPIs: sign-up rate by source; sign-up → first deposit rate (target >35%, under 24h); repeat use within 30 days (target >40%).

## Phases
- **Phase 0:** 0 → 150 active users. Founder-style onboarding, referrals.
- **Phase 1:** 150 → 1,000. Two channels that convert to deposits at low cost.
- **Phase 2:** 1,000+. Partnerships and paid ads, only after sign-up → deposit is above 30%.

## 12-week plan (Oct 12 – Dec 28, 2026)
WFAU targets by week: 3, 6, 10, 15, 20, 26, 32, 38, 44, 50, 55, 60.
Volume target = WFAU × $150 a week (assumption).

## Funnel
Visit → Sign-up → KYC done → First deposit → First trade → Repeat within 30 days → Referred someone

## Channels
| Channel | Phase | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Founder-led onboarding (WhatsApp/Telegram group) | 0 | P1 |
| Referral program (paid after first deposit) | 0–1 | P1 |
| Campus & city ambassadors | 1 | P1 |
| Crypto X/Twitter & Telegram communities | 1 | P2 |
| Partnerships (fintechs, P2P merchants, educators) | 1–2 | P2 |
| SEO & content (deposit guides, rate comparisons) | 1–2 | P2 |
| Paid: crypto ad networks & X retargeting | 2 | P3 |

Verdicts: SCALE at ≥10% sign-up rate, depositors > 0, cost per depositor ≤ $20. STOP at 200+ visits with no depositors. Otherwise TEST.

## First 30-day experiments
| Experiment | Success | Kill rule |
|---|---|---|
| Interview 20 Hashliquid users | 20+ interviews | — |
| Referral reward after first deposit | 15+ referred depositors in 4 weeks | Under 5 after 4 weeks |
| Deposit prompt within 2 minutes of sign-up | +10 points sign-up → deposit | No lift after 2 weeks |
| One campus ambassador pilot | 10+ depositors | Under 3 depositors |

## Retention loop
Welcome series (WhatsApp + email) on days 0/1/3/7 → nudge for a second deposit → monthly leaderboard → win-back after 30 days inactive.

## Pitch
After 4+ logged weeks, the dashboard's Pitch section writes the opening: sign-ups, depositors, volume, WFAU vs plan, best channel. The proposal is a growth lead role for Africa that owns WFAU and volume.
